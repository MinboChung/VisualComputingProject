# Marching tetrahedra: research paper notes

## Contents

1. **"An Efficient Method of Triangulating Equi-Valued Surfaces by Using Tetrahedral Cells" by Akio Doi and Akio Koide (1991):** This seminal paper introduces the Marching Tetrahedra algorithm, addressing ambiguities present in the earlier Marching Cubes method.
